Before you hop in.A few answers.

Do my friends need an account?

No. They open your link, type a name and they're in. There is nothing to install.

Is it really free?

Yes, while it's in beta. It runs on Cloudflare's network, close to wherever your squad plays.

Can I share my game with sound?

Yes, in Chrome and Edge. Turn on Share audio in the picker, or share a single tab to send just its sound.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes for watching, talking and your camera. Phones can't share their screen from a browser, so share from a computer.

Who can get into my room?

Anyone with the link. You're the host when you start it, and you can remove anyone. The room disappears when everyone leaves.
